VarsityKotha (ভার্সিটি কথা) is the daily app for university students in Bangladesh: your class routine every morning, and a safe way to raise campus problems and get them fixed.

YOUR DAY, AT A GLANCE
• Today shows your next class, room and teacher, with a live countdown
• Announcements and upcoming tests in one place
• Works offline: your routine is saved on your phone

ONE ROUTINE FOR YOUR WHOLE SECTION
• Your CR sets up the weekly routine once, and everyone in the section gets it
• Join with a code, or find your class by searching your university's routines
• Taking only some courses? Add just those and hide the rest
• Add personal classes, like a tuition or a club meeting

NEVER MISS A CHANGE
• Cancelled class, new room, new time or moved to another day: your CR updates it in one tap and everyone gets a notification
• Class tests, quizzes, assignments and exams, with dates and topics
• Reminders before every class, and the evening before a test
• A home-screen widget with your next class and next test

CAMPUS VOICE
• Raise problems about classes, halls, transport, food, Wi-Fi and safety
• Anonymous by default: no one, not even your university, can see who wrote it
• Others add "Me too", so the problems that affect the most students rise to the top
• University officials reply in the open and update the status until it's solved
• Photos are cleaned of location details and checked for unsafe content, and posts are moderated

MORE FOR CAMPUS LIFE
• Bus routes: every stop, and the next bus from yours
• CGPA calculator on the UGC 4.00 scale, with a target planner
• Teacher evaluations, university rankings and more are planned

MADE FOR STUDENTS
• Sign in with your university email, or with Google
• English and Bangla, with a dark theme
• No ads, and we never sell your data
