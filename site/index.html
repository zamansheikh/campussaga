My Class

Your weekly routine, a reminder before every class, and an alert when a class is cancelled or the room changes.

Campus Voice

Raise campus problems safely, see how many classmates have the same problem, and follow them until they are fixed.

Private by design

No ads, no selling data, no tracking analytics. Delete your account any time from the app.

Works with student email from every UGC-approved university in Bangladesh, including Google Workspace university accounts.
